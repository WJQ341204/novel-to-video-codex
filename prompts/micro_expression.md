# 微表情词典

人物**面部与肢体的细小动作**。短片只有 2~3 秒，靠大动作演不了戏，全靠微表情。
用法：`promptbank.get("micro_expression", "eyes_narrow")`。

一次只挑 **1~2 个**，堆多了模型会崩脸。

## 眼神

- eyes_narrow: eyes narrowing slightly, gaze sharpening —— 眯眼，审视/警觉
- eyes_lift: slowly lifting the eyes, gaze turning sharp —— 抬眼，从随意转认真
- eyes_dart: eyes darting to the side, avoiding contact —— 闪躲，心虚
- eyes_widen: eyes widening briefly, pupils dilating —— 瞳孔微张，惊觉
- eyes_down: lowering the eyes, lashes trembling —— 垂眼，隐忍
- gaze_lock: steady unblinking gaze locked on the other person —— 盯住不眨眼，压迫

## 嘴角与唇

- lips_press: lips pressing into a thin line —— 抿唇，克制/不悦
- lip_curl: one corner of the mouth curling up, smirk —— 单边嘴角上扬，冷笑
- lips_part: lips parting slightly as if to speak —— 微启唇，欲言
- bite_lip: biting the lower lip briefly —— 咬唇，紧张/犹豫

## 呼吸与喉咙

- breath_hitch: a single sharp intake of breath, shoulders rising slightly —— 吸气一顿，惊
- swallow: visible swallow, adam's apple moving —— 吞咽，紧张
- exhale_slow: slow exhale, shoulders dropping —— 缓缓呼气，放松/放弃

## 手与肢体

- fingers_twitch: fingers twitching slightly at the side —— 手指微动，戒备
- hand_grip: hand tightening on the object, knuckles whitening —— 握紧，发怒/决心
- finger_trace: fingertip tracing the edge of an object absentmindedly —— 无意识摩挲（叶玄机摩挲铜扣）
- shoulder_tense: shoulders tensing up slightly —— 肩一紧，警觉
- head_turn_slow: turning the head slowly toward the sound —— 缓缓转头
- step_back_half: taking half a step back involuntarily —— 不由退半步，退缩

## 额与眉

- brow_rise: one eyebrow rising slightly —— 挑眉，意外/讥讽
- brow_knit: brows knitting together slightly —— 皱眉，困惑/不悦
- temple_vein: a vein visible at the temple, jaw tightening —— 青筋+咬肌，盛怒
